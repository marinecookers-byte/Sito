Induction · Microwave · Grill · Convection

Induction cookers with a 31-litre combination microwave oven

A two or three-zone induction hob and, beneath it, an oven that does three things: microwave, grill and convection, on their own or together. Gimballed, in stainless steel, handmade in Conegliano, Italy.

2 or 3 zones31 litresStainless steelMade in Italy

Where the galley has no room for a separate hob and oven, and a conventional electric oven would ask too much of the boat's electrical system, the combination oven does it all in a single cavity: it heats in minutes with the microwave, browns with the grill and genuinely cooks with convection up to 230 °C. Two versions, one oven: only the hob changes.

The combination oven

Three ways to cook in a single cavity

The microwave to heat and reheat quickly, the grill to brown and crisp, convection to bake as a proper oven does. Each can be used on its own or in combination, and the flat floor, with no turntable, leaves room to cook on two levels.
